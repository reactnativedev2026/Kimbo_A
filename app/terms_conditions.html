3. Payments, Billing & Offline Transactions

Please review our payment disclosure to understand our app operations clearly:

No In-App Invoices or Payments Processing

The SBBMS application is strictly a ledger and loyalty rewards tracking system. There are no features to enter payment credentials, pay bills online, or process cash or card payments directly within the app.

Offline Settlement: All payments for building supplies, deliveries, logistics, and material costs are handled and paid entirely offline or through pre-negotiated invoice payment options directly with our office. The app displays products, pricing estimates, and earned rewards but does not handle actual monetary transfers.
